Quest Home Switcher

A little shortcut to your favourite places.

Open QHS ↗

Keep it in your Library

  1. Open this page in Meta Quest Browser on your headset.
  2. Use the browser’s Add to Library or Add shortcut option, then confirm.
  3. Choose Quest Home Switcher in your Library, then Open QHS.

The option’s name and position can vary with your browser version.

Not opening?

Install a shortcut-enabled QHS version first: 2.1.6-rc.2 or newer. Adding this shortcut does not install the Android app.

If the browser asks to open the app, allow it. If no app opens, use Unknown Sources to open QHS and check its version.

No Library option? Check for a Meta Quest Browser update. You can still bookmark this page.

Just a launcher. Your Homes and settings stay in QHS.
